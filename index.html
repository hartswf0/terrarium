The proposition

“The world is not a backdrop. It is the record of what happens inside it.”

Terrarium keeps motion, accumulation, and accountable change distinct. That lets a world move freely without confusing every passing event with history—and lets repetition become history when it begins to alter the ground.

Terrarium grows where these systems meet.
