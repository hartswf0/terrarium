RELATIONSHIP

There is one ground. Everybody's foot and Argos's paw ask the same terrain what they touch. The trailer's rendered walls, its collision, and its openings derive from one element table. The ball he chases is the ball you threw.

Contact drives motion. Walking requires feet. No paws down means no dog translation. Embodiment is not an animation style; it is a constraint on action.

The world remembers. Feeding, calling, walking, playing fetch, leaving, and returning accumulate. Save. Reload. The place, the actors, and the history remain.

The research

Attachment, when the other can say no

A character that always obeys can demonstrate responsiveness. A character that could refuse but chooses to return may produce something closer to relationship. HLIÐARENDI is built to make that proposition testable — through recognition, repetition, embodiment, memory, place, partial opacity, and voluntary response.
