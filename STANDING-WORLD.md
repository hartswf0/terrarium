# ONE STANDING WORLD
### the harmonized theory-of-the-program
*(CREO × Thunder Rigs × Henry House × Terrarium — one theory beneath four projections)*

---

## 0 · Why a harmonization is possible at all

Four programs, written separately, converged on the same discovery from four
directions:

- **CREO** discovered it from *language*: an utterance is an operation upon an
  already-existing world, never permission for a model to invent one.
  (`<seeing> ≠ <changing>`, `<asking> ≠ <proposing>`, `<proposing> ≠
  <committing>`, `<simulating> ≠ <observing>`.)
- **Thunder Rigs** discovered it from *society*: a world is not scenery but
  accumulated certified operations; rendering, physics, AI, network, history
  and games are views of one CITY; the primitive is "[perform] a certified
  operation on a standing world."
- **Henry House** discovered it from *evidence*: the house does not get placed
  on the land — **the land interrogates the house**; every visible proposal
  must trace backward from rendered consequence to operation to evidence to
  source; derived never masquerades as surveyed.
- **Terrarium** discovered it from *time*: one world lived at three speeds —
  weather (60 Hz, unaccountable), deed (rare, journaled), trace (repetition
  hardening into commitment).

These are not four theories. They are four projections of one theory, and the
proof is that each program's central defect is the *same* defect: somewhere, an
act reached the world without passing through the world's own judgment
(Henry House's 252° bearing bypassing candidate scoring; unset's early AI
output entering the scene unforged; creo-unset-04's houses placing with zero
verdict; every UI whose buttons and words execute different code).

## 1 · <Initial Interpretation>

The harmonized program is a **<standing-world instrument>**:

> `<standing world> [interrogates] <act> [and only certified acts] [become]
> <history> [which becomes] <the standing world>`

The real-world activity it describes is universal: people gathered around a
place they did not fully make — a parcel, a district, an arena, a film set —
asking *what must be true for this to belong here?* The program's job is to
make that question answerable, refusable, reversible, and shareable.

The one governing sentence, generalized from Henry House:

> **The world interrogates the act.**

Not: the actor commands the world. Not: the model constitutes the world.
Every act — a glance, a word, a wheel, a model call, a commit — arrives as a
*candidate*, and the standing world holds the right of refusal.

## 2 · <Theory Skeleton>

### The two axes (the harmonization's central structure)

Every entity and every act has a position on **two orthogonal axes**, and the
whole program is the machinery that classifies, gates, and records those
positions.

**AXIS T — the temporal axis** *(from Terrarium)* — how fast, how accountable:

```
<weather>   60 Hz · no history · no author · never touches the world model
            (motion, projectiles, cameras, performances-in-progress, views)
<deed>      rare · journaled · authored · certified · reversible
            (builds, demolitions, testimony, commits, unsettlings)
<trace>     accumulation · weather that EARNS deed-hood by repetition
            (sediment, ruts, pressure, pre-worn roads, normalization)
```

**AXIS E — the epistemic axis** *(from Henry House and CREO)* — how known:

```
<observed>   surveyed, measured on site — the highest authority
<imported>   official sources with provenance (OSM, parcels, DEM, aerial)
<inferred>   derived by computation (parcels from morphology, road graphs)
<proposed>   candidate acts awaiting adjudication (forge output, drafts)
<simulated>  temporary consequence (rain, flood, playback) — never promoted
<committed>  a proposal that passed the gate and entered history
```

**THE GATE** stands at the crossing of the axes. It is the one place where an
act may change stratum: a `<proposed>` thing becomes `<committed>` only by
passing certification against the standing world; `<weather>` becomes `<deed>`
only through `<trace>` accumulation or explicit commitment. Every parent
implements the gate under a different name — CREO's proposal panel, unset's
FORGE + WORLDCERT + `canPlace`, Henry House's candidate evaluation. They are
one organ.

### <states> (the harmonized loop)

```
PERCEIVE → ATTEND → UTTER → INTERPRET → PROPOSE → ADJUDICATE
   ↑                                                  ↓
   ←── INHABIT ←── SEDIMENT ←── COMMIT ←──┤  or  REFUSE (with named reasons)
```

The recursion is the program: a changed world produces new perception, new
description, new operation. Not "drive around and build things" but
*perceive → describe → operate → inhabit → perceive again.*

### <invariants> — THE EIGHT LAWS

**L1 · One world, many views.** Rendering, physics, AI context, network,
history, games, films are projections of one model. A fork between them is the
program's gravest bug class. `<camera-change> ≠ <world-change>`.

**L2 · The world interrogates the act.** Prompts propose; geometry decides.
Evidence constrains hypothesis. No act reaches the world except through the
gate, and the gate's refusals are named in world terms, never generic errors.

**L3 · Saying is not committing.**
`<utterance> → <interpretation> → <proposal> → <certificate> → <commit>`,
never `<utterance> → <mutation>`. This holds for humans, models, buttons,
chips, cartridges, and twin beats identically — one BUS, one operation
representation, so a button is just another way of speaking.

**L4 · Every commitment carries its cause.** Actor, time, evidence, prompt,
and — where the thing was generated — its *generative code*. Synchronize and
serialize the cause, not the flattened result, whenever flattening would
destroy meaning. An object nobody can answer "who put this here, from what,
can I undo it?" about is ontologically incomplete.

**L5 · Speed is a stratum, not an exception.** Weather never mutates the
model; deeds are never spammed at weather rates; trace is the only lawful
bridge between them. (This law is what reconciles CREO's "change is rare" with
unset's "change is 60 Hz": they were never in conflict — they are different
strata of one world.)

**L6 · Derived never masquerades as observed.** Simulation is not fact; AI
output may hold `<inferred>`, `<proposed>`, `<simulated>` — never `<observed>`
or `<imported>`. Missing evidence becomes a named `<missing-data-condition>`,
never invented certainty. Display simplification (mobile fidelity, LOD) may
reduce representation fidelity but must never change decision fidelity.

**L7 · The world survives the removal of any interpreter.** Remove the model:
deterministic interpreters, golden floors, and manual tools keep every
downstream operation alive. Remove a game: the world remains. If removing the
LLM destroys the program, the LLM had become the ontology — a failed test.

**L8 · Attention is the unit of meaning.** A selection, a drawn region, a
rig's position, an anchor coordinate — these define what "this," "here," "it"
mean next. The subject persists until replaced. Context narrows before
intelligence expands. And — from the Unsettled Atlas — *entering changes the
place; the instruments alter what they measure*: attention itself is an act
the world records.

## 3 · <Assumption Ledger>

- <safe> The four parent programs are projections of one theory — demonstrated
  above by the shared gate, shared laws, and shared defect class.
- <safe> CREO's primitives (journal, authors, branches, say, certification)
  are lawful native implementations of unset's inventions (transactions,
  provenance, unsettle, testimony, cityCert).
- <uncertain> Whether one *codebase* should serve all projections, or one
  *theory* across several codebases with conforming gates. (The theory only
  requires the latter.)
- <uncertain> Whether trace-accumulation thresholds (when do ruts become a
  path-deed?) can be set universally or are per-world policy.
- <requires-user-decision> Which projection is the flagship: the civic
  instrument (CREO), the inhabited commons (Terrarium), or the adjudication
  tool (Henry House). The theory survives any choice; the build order doesn't.

## 5 · <Failure Description> — the harmonized defect taxonomy

Every serious failure in all four programs is one of six theory violations:

1. **Ontology fork** (violates L1) — render says it exists, physics disagrees,
   the network holds a third version.
2. **Uncertified mutation** (L2, L3) — an act reached the world without a
   verdict: the AGENT bar that pipes to a text input; the house placed inside
   a building; early AI output entering the scene raw.
3. **Split language** (L3) — a button and a sentence performing different
   implementations of "the same" act.
4. **Orphan geometry** (L4) — a thing with no answer to "who, why, from what,
   undo?"
5. **Masquerade** (L6) — simulation displayed as fact; registered-from-
   screenshot displayed as surveyed; a coarser mobile search silently
   recommending a different site.
6. **Theory fracture** (all laws) — *two incompatible theories cohabiting one
   program*: Henry House scoring candidates by terrain-fit while rendering a
   fixed 252° bench; creo-unset-04 shipping unset's cockpit without unset's
   constitution. The fracture test: state each subsystem's implied theory in
   one sentence; if two sentences contradict, the program is fractured even if
   no test fails.

The correct response to failure is always *world-termed refusal*: name the
missing evidence, the blocking entity, the violated law — never "something
went wrong," never silent substitution.

## 6 · <Change Test>

**A — Swap the domain** (arena → parcel → city block → film set): only
`{evidence adapters}` and `{evaluators}` change. `<gate>`, `<history>`,
`<BUS>`, `<strata>`, the eight laws survive untouched. (Henry House's
Scenario C, unset's Scenario C, and CREO's Scenario C are the same scenario —
which is itself evidence for the harmonization.)

**B — Remove the model** (L7's test): deterministic interpreters + golden
floors + manual tools carry every operation. Survives by construction.

**C — Go collaborative**: `<actor>` becomes `<participant>`; the gate becomes
the merge rule; provenance becomes distributed. Nothing else moves — and
CREO's branches give the merge model unset never had.

**D — A survey arrives / better evidence lands**: evidence replaces evidence;
old candidates become `<superseded hypotheses>`; committed history is
re-adjudicated against the new ground, not silently rewritten. The program
survives its own correction.

## 10 · <Residual Human Theory>

The code can classify acts, hold verdicts, and keep causes. It cannot decide:

- **what counts as belonging** — the gate can refuse a collision; it cannot
  know whether a lawful tower is a good neighbor;
- **when repetition *should* harden into normal** — the pressure meter
  displays normalization; whether the normal deserves questioning is the
  Unsettled Atlas's question to a person, not a threshold;
- **who holds the right of refusal in company** — certification is mechanical,
  legitimacy is social;
- **which world the branch-keeper loves** — unsettle exposes alternatives;
  choosing among remembered worlds is not an operation.

A future maintainer must understand one sentence above all, because every
convenient shortcut erodes it first:

> **The world is the authority, the act is the candidate, and history is the
> only door between them. Anything that lets an act skip the door — a helpful
> default, a direct mutation, a model given the pen — is not a feature. It is
> the beginning of the program forgetting its own theory.**
