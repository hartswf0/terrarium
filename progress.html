DIRECTION CHANGE, round 3. The client played the working build and found it incoherent: an amber instruction, a red fire line, a narrator line and an open detail card on screen at once, three of them paraphrasing each other, with the open card describing a different failure than the one burning. The answer is not less information but MORE INFORMATION EMBODIED IN THE WORLD. AESTHETIC.md is now the governing law: CIVIC NATURALISM, two registers — the colony is human and tactile, the instrumentation is spare and diagrammatic. Rigor is not discarded, it is moved out of the foreground. The new law, and the eleventh scored criterion, is one question: WHAT FACT DOES THIS ALLOW ME NOT TO WRITE? Retired: the black-and-white palette, the no-border-radius ban, the permanent 28-cell table, the permanent six-button tool bar. Kept: one self-contained file, all 28 real detectors, no emoji, IBM Plex Mono, and the outbreak loop, whose mechanics the client called good — only its presentation changes. The chain was red thing, code, sentence, table, concept, answer. It becomes incident, recognition, intervention.
